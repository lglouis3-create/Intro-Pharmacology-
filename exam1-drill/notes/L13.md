- Botulinum: she said "It's an aerobic bacteria" and in the same sentence "it doesn't need oxygen". The organism is anaerobic; the bank keys the need-no-oxygen point and marks the question low yield.

- 2-AAF at 33 months: she read the dose as "about 45 mg per kilogram" while the axis is in parts per million. The bank carries no 33-month number; it keys the direction (lower threshold, higher cumulative dose).
- Probit panel: "Nobody ever uses this." Not in the bank.
