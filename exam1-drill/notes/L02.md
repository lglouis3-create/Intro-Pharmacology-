## Conflicts / uncertain

- **Inverse agonist affinity:** slide 35 says "Has greater affinity for inactive receptors"; slide 36 says "Has highest affinity for inactive receptors". Slides 37–38 also give irreversible antagonists "(Highest affinity)". The questions avoid ranking inverse agonist against irreversible antagonist affinity.

"my partial agony is creating a signal that is producing 100% of the response" means full agonist. "GDP ... GDP" in several places means GDP off / GTP on. - **Slide 29 vs transcript on calcium:** the slide lists Ca++ as a second messenger; he says not to pick "the calcium channel". These are consistent (ion versus channel), and L02-019 keys Ca++ as a second messenger.
