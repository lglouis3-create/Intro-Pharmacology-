## Conflicts / uncertain

- **Slide numbers are approximate** (see Slide map method). The PDF text export has no page breaks and the PDF could not be downloaded (Drive session expired on three attempts).
- **ADME and "pharmacological" potency:** slide 27 puts age/absorption/distribution/elimination/DDI under *apparent* potency, but in class he asked "does Adne affects the pharmacological potency of a drug? Yes or no? Yes." L01-015 keys the slide framing (route/first-pass → apparent potency) and avoids asking whether ADME affects "pharmacological" potency.
- **Occupancy theory and affinity:** transcript says a full agonist "has the greatest affinity" and a partial agonist "doesn't have a lot of affinity"; slide 28 says "Full agonist & Partial agonist ≠ Affinity". L01-019 keys only the occupancy explanation (partial agonist occupies part of the receptors) and does not ask about affinity differences.
- **Intrinsic activity range for partial agonists:** transcript is garbled ("between 1 and 0.99 ... between 1% and 99% is your partial"). L01-020 asks only the antagonist value (zero), which is clear.
- **Loratadine vs diphenhydramine and the blood–brain barrier:** he said "one can cross the blood vein barrier and the other one doesn't" without naming which; not used in questions.
- **Slides 37–38 PollEV:** not asked in class; keys inferred from his spoken definitions of affinity and efficacy.
- **Slides 39–56 (ion channels, GPCR coupling):** not lectured on 9/22; questions L01-028 to L01-035 are slide-only (L01-032 also uses the transcript GPCR 60% statement).
