- CYP2C19 *17 and a prodrug: "Can you think of a medication that we talked about earlier this morning that could be impacted by A gain of function mutation like this. ... I'm thinking prodrug. ... clopidogrel is a prodrug." (L19-028, L19-029).
- NAT2 monitoring: "which people might we be most concerned about monitoring for hepatotoxicity if they're on isoniazide?" Answer: fast acetylators (Japanese population mostly fast; about half of Caucasians). (L19-012, L19-013).
- TPMT: "what happens to the metabolism of azathioprine, and 6 mercaptopurine? Which way does it go?" Answer: more shunted to 6-thioguanine nucleotides. (L19-042).
- Furuta figure: "Which ones had the highest AUC? Yeah, the poor metabolizers ... the poor metabolizers had the highest gastric pH, right?" (L19-030, L19-031).
