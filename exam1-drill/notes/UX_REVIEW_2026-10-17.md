# UX review, 2026-10-17 (site opens on Exam 3)

Read-only review of `PHAR4344_Exam1_Drill.html` (built Oct 17) in headless Chromium at 1280×900, 820×1180 (iPad) and 390×844 (phone), light and dark, on a fresh profile and after a 15-answer session. Screenshots and scripts: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Intro-Pharmacology-/b3094785-cacf-58b3-9d24-8d3cafd9359e/scratchpad/ux/`. No page errors on any tab at any width. Recommendations are ranked by one question: given what the site already does, what specific change would make it easier to use and learn from?

## Current student experience

A student opening the site today lands on Topics with Exam 3 selected, a 6-day countdown, a folded "What's new" card, "Study what is due", and three Day cards (43 subtopic drills). The quiz works well: per-option "why", verdict, teach paragraph, a folded "More" with her quotes and cites, "Explain more" chips that jump into the Guide, Reference or Tell apart section, and a floating "Back to the question" that restores the exact question and scroll position (verified: L14-032 → Guide §11 → back to L14-032, still answered). Weak spots fills after one session with concept cards naming the option chosen, why it is wrong, the answer and Practice/Read links. The Exam simulator draws 25/50/all from 190 eligible Exam 3 items, scales the clock and reports by topic and skill. Terms has 46 Exam 3 terms with glossary, flashcards and quiz.

The weakest part is orientation on the phone: 16 tabs in a strip four screens wide, with Dr. Smith's words (Guides, Reference, Tell apart) on screens three and four, and six tabs that hold nothing for Exam 3. Two texts on the first screen are stale. One link rule sends 81 of the 119 Exam 3 term questions to the wrong guide section.

## Strengths to preserve

- Explain-more chips plus the floating Back button (`jump`, `RET`, `backBtn` in app.js). All 52 Exam 3 link targets exist.
- Every Exam 3 item has a teach, a cite, per-option whys; 282 of 300 carry her verbatim quote.
- Weak spots concept cards and "Retry the 10 missed this session".
- Exam 2 step-through figures: Back/Next/Replay/Play all at 36 px, narration select per figure, transcript, reduced-motion rule, no label under 11 px on a phone. Script e2-nmj step 1 names exactly the labels drawn. No change needed.
- Scenes: Pause, Replay, speed and narration per scene; nothing plays until tapped.
- Dark theme: verdicts, chosen/correct options and chips keep contrast (phone-quiz-dark.png).
- Exam simulator: per-topic and per-skill table, "Drill the ones I missed", past attempts.
- Progress: export/import, pace, profile, save-failure warning.

## Recommendations, ranked

### R1. Stale Exam 3 blurb on Topics and Exam sim

1. **Where:** `src/course.js` line 78, `exams[2].blurb`; shown on Topics (first paragraph) and the Exam sim start page.
2. **Works:** The blurb states the date, length, her format words and source hierarchy.
3. **Problem:** It ends "In the bank so far: the 10/9 lecture only; the 10/13 and later lectures are added as their decks and transcripts arrive." The "What's new" card directly beneath says Day 14 and Day 15 were added (300 questions, 290 shown in the bank count).
4. **Effect:** The first sentence a returning student reads contradicts the card under it; a student may skip the two newest lectures or distrust the counts.
5. **Change:** Replace the sentence with "In the bank: 10/9, 10/13 and 10/14; 10/19 and 10/20 are added as their transcripts arrive." Also `gen_terms.py` line 162 hard-codes the Terminology card's cite "Definitions from the Day 1–13 decks and transcripts"; make it "Day 1–15" or derive it from `COURSE.lectures`.
6. **Alternatives:** Generate the "in the bank" line from `COURSE.lectures` with `exam:3` so it never goes stale.
7. **Priority high; effort 10 minutes (generated version 30); no dependencies.**
8. **Verify:** Topics first paragraph and the Terminology card name 10/13 and 10/14; `node test.js`.

### R2. Phone tab strip: 16 tabs, four screens, no hint, six empty for Exam 3

1. **Where:** `VIEWS` and `nav()` in `src/app.js`; `#nav` CSS in `shell.html`.
2. **Works:** The strip scrolls, keeps the open tab in view, and tabs are keyboard-focusable. On iPad and laptop all tabs fit in two rows.
3. **Problem (observed):** At 390 px the strip is 1399 px wide (3.8 screens). Offsets: Graphs 371, Reference 795, Guides 1168, Progress 1239. `#nav` has no mask, gradient or `::after`; the only sign of more tabs is a clipped letter ("G") at the right edge. While studying Exam 3, Graphs, Diagrams, Scenes, All drugs, Pair up and Drug ↔ receptor show only "added as its lectures are written" or Exam 2 content, yet sit between Exam sim and Guides. Topics' Day cards carry no link to the Guide or Reference (0 jump links in the Exam 3 group), so on a phone the only ways to Dr. Smith's words are three swipes of the strip or answering a question first.
4. **Effect:** A student on a phone may not know Guides, Reference and Tell apart exist for Exam 3; the tabs most likely to be tapped by mistake are empty.
5. **Change:** (a) Order and group tabs by the exam being studied: put the tabs with content for the active exam first, and move tabs with no content for it (`Graphs`, `Diagrams`, `Scenes`, `All drugs`, `Pair up`, `Drug ↔ receptor` for Exam 3) to the end of the strip with a muted style, not hidden. (b) Add a fade at the strip's right edge while `scrollLeft + clientWidth < scrollWidth`, cleared at the end. (c) Add a "Read" chip to each Day heading on Topics using `BY_LECTURE3`/`BY_LECTURE2` (`data-jump="guide:guide3-l15-1"`), so each lecture's guide is one tap from where its drills are.
6. **Alternatives:** A two-row wrapping strip (as on the iPad) costs 36 px of header on every page and still shows the empty tabs. A "More" menu hides functions. Demoting rather than hiding keeps Exam 2 tools one scroll away.
7. **Priority high; effort 2–3 hours; depends on nothing. (c) alone is 30 minutes.**
8. **Verify:** Phone screenshot of each tab shows the fade when more tabs exist; with Exam 3 active, Guides and Reference are on the first or second screen; every tab still reachable; browser_test.js nav loop passes.

### R3. Term questions link to the wrong Exam 3 guide section

1. **Where:** `TERM_SUB3` and `linksFor0()` in `src/app.js` (Exam 3 branch: `if (q.topic === 'TERMS') return [TERM_SUB3, ['ref','ref3-1',...]]`).
2. **Works:** Exam 2 term questions map by glossary group (`TERM_SUB2`), and the 15 Toxicology-principles terms correctly reach Guide §1 and Reference 3.1.
3. **Problem (observed):** All 119 Exam 3 term questions resolve to `guide3-1` ("Definitions: poison, toxin, toxicant...") and `ref3-1` ("the words"), including 37 L14 and 44 L15 term questions from the Toxic responses and Treating poisonings groups. Example: the neuronopathy item (phone-quiz-right.png) offers "Guide: definitions" although its section is Guide §16 Neurotoxicity. Terms are 40% of the Exam 3 bank, so this affects 2 in 5 served questions.
4. **Effect:** The chip promises the explanation and lands on the wrong page; a student stops using the chips on term items.
5. **Change:** Key `TERM_SUB3` by glossary group like `TERM_SUB2`: `g12 → guide3-1/ref3-1`, `g13 → guide3-l14-4` (toxic responses) with per-term overrides for lung/nerve/liver/kidney/immune (`guide3-l15-1…6`), `g14 → guide3-l15-7` (treating poisonings); point the Reference link at the matching `ref3-l14-*`/`ref3-l15-*` table.
6. **Alternatives:** Add a `guide` field to each glossary term (precise, 46 edits) rather than a group map (coarse, 3 edits).
7. **Priority high; effort 1 hour; add the anchor check from ENGINE_UPGRADES ("every LINKS anchor exists") to `test.js`.**
8. **Verify:** `QUESTIONS.filter(q => examOf(q)===3 && q.skill==='term').map(linksFor)` yields at least four distinct guide anchors; the neuronopathy item links to §16.

### R4. "Study what is due" serves definitions 40% of the time

1. **Where:** `pickSR()` in `src/app.js` (unseen branch `shuffle(unseen)[0]`); Topics skill chips.
2. **Works:** Missed concepts return at once in another wording; the exam draw caps term items at 15% (`nTerm`); the skill chips let the student pick one skill.
3. **Problem (observed):** On a fresh Exam 3 profile, five runs of 30 served questions gave 9–14 term items each (30–47%), matching the bank share (119 of 300), not the exam share (≤15%, and her exam is written from objectives and lecture words). The skill chips allow "Terms only" but not "everything except Terms".
4. **Effect (hypothesis):** A student who uses the default button spends almost half of drill time on one-line definitions. Whether this harms the score is untested; the share mismatch is the observed fact.
5. **Change:** In the unseen branch, draw a term item with probability ≈0.2 while non-term unseen remain (one line, keeps the random order otherwise). Add an "All but Terms" skill chip, or let skill chips multi-select.
6. **Alternatives:** Leave the picker and rely on the Exam sim's cap; cheaper but the default path stays term-heavy.
7. **Priority medium-high; effort 1 hour; no dependencies.**
8. **Verify:** Rerun `dist.js` (scratchpad): term share of 30 served unseen items falls to 15–25% across five runs; `browser_test.js` due-quiz loop passes.

### R5. Exam 3 format practice: short answer and no going back

1. **Where:** Exam sim (`vExam`, `renderExam`), Exam 3 blurb and Guide 3 scope section.
2. **Works:** The scope section quotes her: "they may not all be multiple choice... very short answer essays... fill in the blank" and "no backwards". Flashcards already practise term recall before the answer shows.
3. **Problem (observed):** The Exam 3 bank is 296 multiple-choice and 4 matching items; none asks for an answer before options appear. The simulator shows Previous and the number strip, which her exam will not.
4. **Effect (hypothesis):** Recognition practice only; on a fill-in item the student has never produced the words unprompted.
5. **Change:** A "Recall first" chip in the quiz header that hides the options behind "Show options"; the student says or writes the answer, then grades as usual. A "No going back (as she runs it)" checkbox on the Exam 3 start card that disables Previous and the strip.
6. **Alternatives:** True short-answer items with free-text grading are larger and error-prone; the hide-options chip gets most of the value with no grading.
7. **Priority medium; effort 2 hours; no dependencies.**
8. **Verify:** With the chip on, no `[data-o]` renders until "Show options" is tapped; keyboard letters still work after reveal; an Exam 3 paper with the checkbox has no enabled Previous.

### R6. Small touch targets on the phone

1. **Where:** `#nav button` (62×28), `[data-layout]` chips (90×24), Explain-more chips (115×26), "Not sure"/"I guessed" (71×25), stepper dots `.sdot` (12×12, no padding) in `shell.html`.
2. **Works:** Options (≥39 px), Flag (36), Next (35), stepper Back/Next/Play (36) are comfortable.
3. **Problem (observed):** The listed controls are under the 44 px iOS guideline and some under 28 px; the dots are the only way to go to a specific step and are 12 px.
4. **Effect:** Mis-taps on chips that sit in a row (Explain more, confidence) and on dots; the tab strip is both small and scrolling.
5. **Change:** `min-height:36px` on `.chip`, `[data-c]`, `#nav button` at ≤560 px; give `.sdot` a 32×32 hit area with padding and keep the 12 px visual.
6. **Alternatives:** None needed.
7. **Priority medium; effort 30 minutes; no dependencies.**
8. **Verify:** Re-run the `TARGETS`/`TARGETS2` measurements in `journey2.js`; all listed controls report height ≥36.

### R7. Topics page length on the phone for Exam 3

1. **Where:** `vTopics()`; Exam 3 group (43 cards, 6,404 px tall at 390 px; page 7,186 px).
2. **Works:** "Study what is due" and the skill chips sit above the fold; each card shows seen/accuracy.
3. **Problem (observed):** Each subtopic card takes a full-width block with a Drill button; Day 15 alone is 15 cards. The Day heading has no drill button of its own, so "drill this lecture" needs a scroll through its cards.
4. **Effect:** Reaching Day 15 is 20 screens of scrolling; the most recent lecture is the hardest to start.
5. **Change:** Fold each Day under its heading (closed by default on a phone, as the "What's new" card is), with a "Drill all of Day N" button and the Read chip from R2(c) in the heading. Keep the cards unchanged inside.
6. **Alternatives:** A two-column grid of cards at 390 px halves the length but shrinks the touch targets.
7. **Priority medium-low; effort 1–2 hours; pairs with R2(c).**
8. **Verify:** Exam 3 group under 1,500 px tall on the phone with Days closed; `[data-topic]` drills still open from inside each fold.

### R8. Pronoun and empty-state wording on Exam 3

1. **Where:** `src/app.js` line 2523 (`'His figures for this exam are added as they come up in class.'`), shown on Graphs with Exam 3 open.
2. **Works:** Other empty states are neutral ("This exam's diagrams are added as its lectures are written").

4. **Effect:** Small; the site elsewhere is careful about whose words are quoted.

7. **Priority low; effort 2 minutes.**

### R9. Audio and animation: no change for Exam 2; none to add for Exam 3 yet

- **Exam 2 narration and steppers (Diagrams, Guides, Scenes, All drugs walkthrough):** controls, per-figure narration select, transcript, speed and reduced-motion are present; the e2-nmj and e2-sch scripts match the step shown and add no facts. Playback timing, clip loading and the device-voice fallback cannot be judged in headless Chromium. Classified: already effective.
- **Exam 3 (L13–L15):** no figures, graphs, images or clips (0 of 300 items carry `img`/`graph`/`fg`); the guide sections are quote-dense prose (phone-guide3-l15.png). Candidates for a static figure, if her slides carry one: liver zones with the oxygen gradient and CYP2E1 (Guide §18) and overdose kinetics (Guide §21). No animation: these are static relationships. No audio until the student asks for a specific section; the quotes and chips already carry her words. Classified: missing, usefulness unproven.

## Navigation summary

Where you are and where to go are clear on iPad and laptop (two-row tab bar, open tab marked, Back button after a jump). On the phone they are not (R2, R6, R7). Fewer tabs is not recommended; exam-aware ordering, a scroll hint and per-Day Read chips keep every function and shorten the common paths.

## Proposed pilot

1. Apply R1, R3 and R8 (under an hour), rebuild, run `test.js`, `style_check.js`, and the scratchpad scripts `journey2.js` (link targets, touch sizes) and `dist.js` (term share).
2. Apply R2(b) and R2(c) only (fade plus Read chips), then study on the phone for one session and note: taps from Topics to Guide §15 (now three swipes plus a tap; target one tap), and whether an Explain-more chip on a term item lands on the right section.
3. If the term share still feels high after a week of "Study what is due", apply R4 and compare the per-skill table of two 50-item simulated papers before and after.

Audio playback, pronunciation and clip timing; real iPad Safari; `browser_test.js` (not run); whether any recommendation raises exam scores, which needs the student's own trial.
