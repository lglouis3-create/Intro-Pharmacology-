# Stem review: ask the way he asks, give nothing away

Edit the bank files in `/home/user/Intro-Pharmacology-/exam1-drill/src/` (q_L01.js … q_L06.js, q_POLLS.js, q_JEOP.js, and gen_druglist.py for the DL1 items, never q_DL1.js or q_TERMS.js directly). Nothing else.

## The two problems to fix
1. **Stems that hand over the fact the question tests.** On his exam the student is expected to know each drug-list drug's class and receptor; he names the drug and asks for the consequence. Example of what to fix: "Metoprolol is a reversible β1 receptor antagonist, and β1 receptors are located in the heart. What effect would it have on heart rate?" → "What effect would metoprolol have on heart rate?" (his 9/22 spoken question was "If I give you metoprolol, which is a beta 1 selective antagonist ... what kind of effect is it going to produce?" when teaching it; on the exam the drug list is assumed). Rule: a stem may name a drug-list drug, a receptor, a tissue and a figure; it may not state the drug's class, mechanism, receptor preference, reversibility or location when any of those is what the options turn on. Drugs NOT on the Exam 1 drug list (aripiprazole, pindolol, buprenorphine, curare, thiazides, Prozac/fluoxetine, duloxetine, physostigmine, carbidopa, milrinone, caffeine, loratadine is ON the list) may keep a short mechanism statement, as he gave one in class ("Since it's not on your drug list, I'm telling you what it is"). Keep the explanation of the mechanism in `teach` and the `why` fields, never in the stem. Exam 1 drug list: norepinephrine, epinephrine, acetylcholine, tropicamide, prazosin, phenylephrine, phenoxybenzamine, albuterol, metoprolol, loratadine, diazepam, varenicline, histamine, and the others in notes/BRIEF.md.
2. **Questions that test course meta-information rather than pharmacology.** Remove items whose answer is a statement about the syllabus or his teaching plan rather than a drug or receptor fact: "which pairing matches the item that must be known with the item that should be known" (L01-001 and any sibling), "what will / will not be tested", "how many questions", "which exam covers". Remove = delete the object from QUESTIONS.push; then fix any `dupOf` pointing at it and any tell.js row `data-q` pointing at it (tell.js may be edited only to retarget such a `data-q` to another existing question on the same row topic, or to delete that row if none fits). The must-know/should-know idea itself may survive as a one-line note in another question's teach where it already is.

## Method
- Grep every stem for drug-list names followed by "is a", "is an", ", a ", ", an ", "(a ", "antagonist", "agonist", "located", "reversible", "selective" and judge each by the rule above. Also check select-all stems and the `why` texts of correct options do not repeat the stem's giveaway.
- Rewrite in his formats (STYLE.md and notes/POLL_BRIEF.md): "What effect would X have on …?", "Which of the following drugs …?", "X is most likely:", "Which statement is CORRECT?", increase / decrease / no effect scenarios.
- Keep ids, keys, options and figures unless the rewrite forces an option change; keep every option's `why`; keep the longest-option rule (the correct option is never uniquely the longest) and the "no because/since in options" rule.
- Verbatim items (tags include 'pollev', sub 'verbatim') are his own wording: do not touch them even when they state a mechanism.

Run from src/: `python3 build.py && node test.js && node style_check.js` until clean. Report: ids rewritten (old stem → new stem, one line each), ids removed and why, anything you judged borderline and left. Do not commit.
