## Conflicts / uncertain
- **CPIC name (L21-014).** She said "the Center for Pharmacogenomics Information Consortium guidelines"; slide p. 16 and the guideline give Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium (the p. 9 title text layer is garbled). Keyed to the slide.
- **Recommendation strength (L21-020).** She said "strong, moderate, or optimal"; slide p. 12 reads "Optional", and the guideline grades recommendations STRONG, MODERATE and OPTIONAL. Keyed to the slide word.
- **Number of CPIC guidelines (L21-022).** She said "I think it's now about 35 guidelines written" and later "about 35"; the p. 13 table has 29 rows (some rows cover several drugs or a drug class). Keyed to her spoken number.
- **Slide p. 13 dates out of date.** She said the table was not updated: warfarin (2017 on the slide) and thiopurines (2018 on the slide) were "just updated"; "Thiopurines, the previous one was 2018. It was just updated 2026." For warfarin her year was unclear ("I'm mixing up my dates"), and she could not open the new warfarin guideline, so the walk-through used the 2017 update. No question keys the new dates.
- **Empirical warfarin dose (L21-028).** She said "Usually we just start with 5 mg per kilogram." On 10/26 she gave about 5 mg/day; the guideline says the initial dose is often based on population averages (e.g., 4-5 mg/day). The number is not keyed.
- **CYP4F2 direction (L21-034, L21-035).** In the walk-through she first said "If they have that snip, you need to decrease the dose again" and, on the African side, "if they have that variant, they need to have their dose further decreased". After a student question she said the CYP4F2 variant needs "an increased dose" and "The SIP4F2 is mentioned for people of non-African ancestry. It's not on the algorithm for the people of African ancestry.". The guideline: on the non-African branch, if CYP4F2*3 is detected, increase the dose by 5-10% (OPTIONAL); for African ancestry, no recommendation is made for CYP4F2. Keyed to her corrected statement and the guideline. She also repeatedly said "2F4" for 4F2 and corrected herself.
- **rs12777823 (L21-035).** She called it "another P450 variant" that is "decreasing the activity of somewhere of the 2C9" and said "They're not really using it yet". The guideline: a non-coding SNP in the CYP2C cluster near CYP2C18, associated with altered warfarin clearance; dose reduction of 10-25% recommended in African Americans with A/G or A/A (MODERATE); most clinical laboratories do not yet include it in panels. Keyed to the guideline wording only where she matched it.
- **CYP2C9*5, *6, *8, *11 reduction (L21-032).** She said "decrease that calculated dose by 15 to 30%"; the guideline says 15-30% per variant allele (20-40% in homozygotes). Keyed to 15-30%.
- **HER2 "185" (label-trast).** The caption is garbled ("185. K8 2345"); she said "I think the 185 is the size of it". The Herceptin label (12.1): HER2 "encodes a transmembrane receptor protein of 185 kDa". She said this year's label was the first she had seen with information on what makes the receptor expressed; the label text available does not show which content is new. Not quoted from the caption.
