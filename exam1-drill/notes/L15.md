## Conflicts and transcription points
- **Gas solubility direction.** She said "very hydrophilic gasses. Will actually go quite far down into the lungs and your hydrophobic gasses do not". The slide says only "Water solubility of gases" under site of deposition. Her statement is the reverse of the usual textbook account (highly water-soluble gases are taken up in the upper airway; poorly soluble ones reach the alveoli). L15-003 is keyed on the property (water solubility) and quotes her direction with a note; flag for the user.
- **Therapeutic index of drugs A and B.** Slide: "two meds may have the same therapeutic index but unequal safe dosing". Transcript: "you've got the same therapeutic index ... but for drug B, because the therapeutic index is different than drug A". L15-050 keys the figure reading she gave (B plateaus at anesthesia; A reaches coma; "Which one can you increase the dose and not have an unwanted adverse effect? B.") and records both wordings in its note.
