## Objectives (slide 2, verbatim)
- Compare and contrast Nn vs Nm receptors
- Differentiate the types, SOA, and MOA
- Describe the peripheral motor nervous system
- Demonstrate cell signaling pathways mediating NMJ
- Identify enzymes-linked systems and the corresponding effectors responses.
- Distinguish the MOA, SOA, DDI and ADR of drugs
